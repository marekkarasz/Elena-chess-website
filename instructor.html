About Elena

Teaching Philosophy

Elena believes chess isnt's about memorizing moves - it's about understanding patterns and thinking clearly. Her goal is to help you see the board the way strong players do, one concept at the time. Too many students get lost in endless opening theory or tactical puzzles without grasping the fundemental principles that tie everything together. She focuses on building your intuition and decision-making skills so you can play confidentely in any position. When you understand the 'why' behind each move, improvements becomes natural and enjoyable.
